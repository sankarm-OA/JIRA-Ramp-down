# Legacy Data Archival and Compliance Storage

## Epic Information
**Epic Name:** Legacy Data Archival and Compliance Storage

**Linked Product Module:** Frontier Toolset (OneStack Roll Out) — Decommissioning Phase

**Linked Product Vision Functional Requirements:**
- Ensure all historical JIRA issues are extracted, validated, and archived per enterprise retention policy
- Ensure all Tempo worklog records are securely encrypted and stored in cold storage
- Maintain audit trail and compliance documentation for data archival activities
- Enable future retrieval of archived data for compliance and legal discovery purposes

## Overview
**Summary:** Extract, validate, encrypt, and archive all historical JIRA issues and Tempo worklog records into secure, compliant cold storage before license termination. Establish verifiable chain of custody and retention metadata to satisfy enterprise data governance and regulatory requirements.

**Problem Statement:** JIRA and Tempo systems contain 5+ years of operational data (issues, worklogs, attachments, audit trails) that must be preserved per enterprise retention policy before systems are decommissioned. Current data lacks standardized archival format, encryption, retention tagging, and retrieval documentation. Risk of non-compliance, data loss, or inability to respond to legal discovery requests if archival is incomplete or untracked.

**MoSCoW Priority:** Must have

## Strategic Context
**ICPs Impacted:**
- Enterprise organizations with strict data retention and compliance requirements
- Regulated industries subject to data governance and legal hold obligations
- Organizations migrating from legacy project management to unified platforms

**Key Personas Supported:**
- Compliance Officer — ensuring archival meets regulatory and policy requirements
- IT Operations Lead — executing archival process and managing cold storage infrastructure
- Data Governance Manager — tracking retention metadata and audit trails
- Legal/Discovery Team — accessing archived data for compliance inquiries

**Business Value:** Eliminates compliance risk from premature data deletion. Reduces operational burden of maintaining dual systems. Provides defensible audit trail for data retention. Protects organization from legal/regulatory penalties. Enables confident license termination and cost closure.

## Success Criteria
- All JIRA issues (5+ years) successfully extracted, validated, and encrypted in cold storage
- All Tempo worklog records (5+ years) indexed, encrypted, and stored with retention metadata
- Archival process documented with verifiable chain of custody
- Retention policy compliance verified by compliance/legal team
- Archive retrieval process tested and documented for future discovery requests
- Archival completion certified and sign-off obtained before license termination
