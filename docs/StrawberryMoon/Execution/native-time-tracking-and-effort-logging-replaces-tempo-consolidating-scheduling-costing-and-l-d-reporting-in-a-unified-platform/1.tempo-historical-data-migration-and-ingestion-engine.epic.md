# Tempo Historical Data&#x20;

## Epic Information

**Epic Name:** Tempo Historical Data Migration and Ingestion Engine

**Linked Product Module:** Time-Tracking and Effort-Logging Module

**Linked Product Vision Functional Requirements:**

* FR-1: Migrate historical effort logs and time entries from Tempo into native platform
* FR-2: Preserve cost-centre allocation history and audit trails
* FR-3: Maintain L\&D reporting continuity across data migration
* FR-4: Validate data integrity and reconcile discrepancies during ingestion

## Overview

**Summary:** Build and execute ETL (extract, transform, load) pipelines to migrate historical Tempo effort logs, time entries, cost-centre allocations, and audit records into the native time-tracking data store. Ensure zero data loss, audit trail preservation, and reporting continuity during the transition from Tempo to native system.

**Problem Statement:** Replacing Tempo with a native time-tracking capability requires migrating years of historical effort logs, cost allocations, and compliance records. Without a robust migration strategy, organizations risk losing audit history, breaking retrospective reporting, and creating gaps in cost-centre tracking. Manual migration is error-prone and unsustainable at scale.

**MoSCoW Priority:** Must have

## Strategic Context

**ICPs Impacted:**

* Enterprise organizations with multi-year Tempo deployments requiring historical data continuity
* Cost-centre and finance teams dependent on historical effort allocation reports
* Compliance and audit functions tracking learning & development investments over time

**Key Personas Supported:**

* Finance Manager: Needs accurate historical cost-centre reporting and audit trails
* Compliance Officer: Requires preserved audit logs and data lineage
* Platform Administrator: Manages data migration, validation, and cutover
* L\&D Lead: Depends on historical learning activity data for reporting and analysis

**Business Value:**

* Eliminates third-party tool dependency and associated licensing costs
* Enables uninterrupted cost-centre and L\&D reporting across historical and future data
* Reduces integration complexity by consolidating data in a single system
* Ensures audit compliance by preserving complete historical records
* Provides clean foundation for native reporting and analytics

## Success Criteria

* 100% of historical Tempo effort logs successfully migrated to native data store with zero loss
* Cost-centre allocations and audit trails preserved and validated against source system
* Automated reconciliation process identifies and flags any discrepancies for resolution
* L\&D reporting functionality demonstrates continuity across pre- and post-migration data
* Cutover executed with zero downtime and minimal end-user disruption
