# Archive the Tempo historical Data for the capitalisation reference

## Epic Information

**Epic Name:** Archive Tempo Historical Data for Capitalisation Reference

**Linked Product Module:** Financial Auditing & Effort Tracking

**Linked Product Vision Functional Requirements:**

* Historical data retention for R\&D tax credit and software capitalisation audits

## Overview

**Summary:** Archive past 3-5 years of historical Tempo time-tracking and worklog records into a compliant, query-able archive to maintain audit trails for software capitalisation and audit purpose.

**Problem Statement:** Phase out Tempo without preserving historical worklog and effort data creates severe compliance and audit exposure for past capitalised R\&D assets and historical tax claims.

**MoSCoW Priority:** Must have

## Strategic Context

**ICPs Impacted:**

* Finance and Accounting Operations
* Engineering Leadership

**Key Personas Supported:**

* Head of Finance / Financial Controller
* Compliance & Audit Lead
* Engineering Operations Manager

**Business Value:** Eliminates vendor lock-in and licensing costs for legacy Tempo while guaranteeing uninterrupted audit compliance for past capitalisation filings.

## Success Criteria

* 100% of historical Tempo worklogs, account links, and user associations extracted and verified.
* Immutable, encrypted archival storage established with defined retention policies (7+ years).
* Finance and audit teams can generate audit reports for any past fiscal period within 24 hours.
* Zero data loss verified against historical financial balance sheet capitalisation schedules.

## Scope

### In Scope

* Full extraction of historical worklogs, accounts, categories, and user mapping from Tempo API/database.
* Data validation, cleansing, and checksum verification against past capitalisation records.
* Ingestion into secure, cost-effective cold storage / data lake with role-based access control.
* Documentation of query scripts and extraction procedures for audit requests.

### Out of Scope

* Active synchronization with Tempo post-decommissioning.
* Real-time querying UI for end engineering users.
* Live modification or editing of archived historical records.
