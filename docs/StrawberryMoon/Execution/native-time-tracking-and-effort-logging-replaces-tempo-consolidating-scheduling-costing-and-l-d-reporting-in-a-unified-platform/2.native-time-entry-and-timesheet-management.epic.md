# Native Time Entry and Timesheet Management

## Epic Information
**Epic Name:** Native Time Entry and Timesheet Management

**Linked Product Module:** Frontier Toolset (OneStack Roll Out) — Time Tracking & Effort Logging

**Linked Product Vision Functional Requirements:**
- FR-1: Replace external Tempo dependency with native time-tracking capability
- FR-2: Preserve cost-centre allocation and reporting
- FR-3: Maintain learning & development activity tracking
- FR-4: Enable consolidated time and effort reporting

## Overview
**Summary:** Build a native time-tracking and timesheet management system that replaces Tempo, delivering intuitive time entry, interactive timesheet views, inline cost-centre allocation, and unified L&D and compliance reporting within the target platform.

**Problem Statement:** Organizations currently depend on Tempo as an external tool for time-tracking and effort-logging, fragmenting data across systems, complicating integrations, and requiring manual reconciliation for cost-centre and L&D reporting. This creates operational friction, increases maintenance burden, and introduces audit and accuracy risks.

**MoSCoW Priority:** Must have

## Strategic Context
**ICPs Impacted:**
- Enterprise organizations with complex cost-centre structures
- Organizations with mandatory L&D and compliance reporting requirements
- Teams requiring real-time resource utilization visibility

**Key Personas Supported:**
- Resource managers and team leads (timesheet oversight, allocation decisions)
- Individual contributors (daily time entry, effort logging)
- Finance and operations teams (cost-centre reporting, compliance)
- L&D coordinators (learning activity tracking)

**Business Value:** Eliminates third-party Tempo licensing costs, reduces integration complexity, improves data accuracy through single-source-of-truth architecture, enables real-time reporting and resource visibility, and streamlines daily workflows by consolidating scheduling, time tracking, and reporting into one platform.

## Success Criteria
- Users can log time entries with cost-centre allocation in under 30 seconds
- Timesheet submission and approval workflows complete with zero manual steps outside the platform
- Cost-centre and L&D reports generate in real-time with 100% data accuracy match to legacy Tempo exports
- System supports bulk time entry import and retroactive entry adjustment
- Permission controls prevent unauthorized cost-centre changes and ensure audit trail
- Platform achieves feature parity with Tempo's core functionality within scope

## Scope
### In Scope
- Native time entry interface with single-click cost-centre allocation
- Interactive timesheet views with daily/weekly/monthly aggregation
- Time validation rules (minimum entry duration, overlap detection, cost-centre restrictions)
- Cost-centre allocation engine with support for multi-allocation
