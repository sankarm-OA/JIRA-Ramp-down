# JIRA and Tempo have historically underpinned engineering backlog management, sprint tracking, workflow management and the majority of engineering metrics reported to the CTO office

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** Frontier Toolset — Engineering Platform Migration
**Version:** 1.0

## Headline
Organization transitions engineering backlog, sprint tracking, and metrics reporting from JIRA and Tempo to Strawberry Moon and OneStack

## Summary
The organization is migrating its engineering operations from JIRA and Tempo to Strawberry Moon and OneStack, consolidating backlog management, sprint tracking, workflow orchestration, and CTO-level metrics into a unified strategic platform. This transition modernizes our engineering tracking infrastructure and improves visibility across the organization.

## Problem Statement
JIRA and Tempo, while functional, have become fragmented touchpoints for engineering data. Teams manage backlogs, sprint cycles, and workflows across disconnected systems, and CTO reporting relies on manual data synthesis across multiple sources. This creates operational friction, inconsistent data models, and limited insight into true engineering capacity and delivery trends.

## Solution
Strawberry Moon and OneStack provide an integrated, purpose-built engineering platform that consolidates backlog management, sprint planning, workflow execution, and metrics generation into a single source of truth. The migration centralizes engineering operations, improves data consistency, and enables real-time visibility for leadership and individual contributor teams alike.

## Key Features
- Unified backlog and sprint planning across all engineering functions
- Native workflow automation aligned to organizational delivery processes
- Real-time metrics and reporting dashboard for CTO office and leadership
- Seamless integration with existing development toolchains and CI/CD pipelines

## Customer Benefits
- Engineering teams gain faster sprint planning cycles and clearer work prioritization
- CTO office receives consolidated, real-time metrics without manual reporting overhead
- Organization achieves single source of truth for engineering capacity, velocity, and delivery trends
- Teams reduce context-switching and administrative burden from multi-tool management

## Customer Quote (Imagined)
> "Migrating to Strawberry Moon and OneStack eliminated the data fragmentation we lived with across JIRA and Tempo. Our sprint planning is now 40% faster, and our CTO finally has the real-time visibility needed to make confident capacity decisions."

## Call to Action
Engineering teams should begin onboarding to Strawberry Moon and OneStack immediately. Contact the Platform Engineering team for migration scheduling, training resources, and backlog import support.

## Internal Notes
This migration is a strategic platform consolidation decision made by leadership in Q3 FY26. Stakeholders include CTO office, all engineering squads, and platform engineering. Data migration planning and cutover timeline should be coordinated with minimal disruption
