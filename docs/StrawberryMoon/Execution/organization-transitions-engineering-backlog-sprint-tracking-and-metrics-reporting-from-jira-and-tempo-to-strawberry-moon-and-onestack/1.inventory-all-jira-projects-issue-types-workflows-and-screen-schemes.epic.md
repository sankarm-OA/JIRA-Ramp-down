# Archive CTO related JIRA projects, issue types, workflows and screen schemes. This will be an Inventory for baseline/historical reference.

## Epic Information

**Epic Name:** Inventory all JIRA projects, issue types, workflows and screen schemes

**Linked Product Module:** Frontier Toolset — Engineering Platform Migration

**Linked Product Vision Functional Requirements:**

* Consolidate backlog management and sprint tracking infrastructure
* Establish single source of truth for engineering data models
* Enable CTO-level metrics reporting without manual synthesis

## Overview

**Summary:** Comprehensive audit and cataloging of all JIRA projects, issue types, workflows, and screen schemes currently in use across the organization. This inventory serves as the foundation for migration planning, data mapping, and cutover validation to Strawberry Moon and OneStack.

**Problem Statement:** Without a complete inventory of JIRA's current configuration, migration teams cannot accurately assess scope, identify data transformation requirements, or validate that all workflows and business logic transfer correctly to the new platform. This gap creates risk of incomplete migration and loss of critical process configurations.

**MoSCoW Priority:** Must have

## Strategic Context

**ICPs Impacted:**

* Engineering teams (all squads)
* CTO office
* Platform Engineering

**Key Personas Supported:**

* Platform Engineering Lead (owns migration execution)
* Engineering Squad Leads (validate workflow capture)
* CTO (approves scope and cutover readiness)

**Business Value:** Eliminates migration blind spots, reduces cutover risk, ensures no workflows or configurations are lost during transition. Enables accurate effort estimation for data mapping and validation phases.

## Success Criteria

* Complete list of all active JIRA projects documented with project key, name, and team ownership
* All issue types across all projects cataloged with field configurations and custom fields identified
* All active workflows documented including state transitions, automation rules, and permission schemes
* All screen schemes and field visibility rules captured and mapped to issue types
* Inventory validated by at least one representative from each engineering squad
* Discrepancies or legacy/unused configurations flagged for decision (migrate vs. retire)

## Scope

### In Scope

* All JIRA Cloud/Server instances used by engineering organization
* Active projects currently in use for backlog, sprint, or workflow management
* All issue types (standard and custom)
* All workflow definitions and associated automation
* Screen schemes, field layouts, and visibility rules
* Custom fields and their configurations
* Project-level permissions and role mappings
* Integration points with CI/CD or other tooling

### Out of Scope

* Historical archived projects (unless required for audit compliance)
* Tempo-specific time tracking configuration (separate effort)
* JIRA plugin ecosystem and third-party app inventory (separate effort)
