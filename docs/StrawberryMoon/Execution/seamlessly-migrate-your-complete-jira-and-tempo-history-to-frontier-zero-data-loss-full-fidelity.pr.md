# Migrate historical issues, epics, sprints, comments, attachments and worklogs from JIRA and Tempo into the target platform(s) without data loss

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** Frontier Toolset (OneStack Roll Out)
**Version:** 1.0

## Headline
Seamlessly migrate your complete JIRA and Tempo history to Frontier — zero data loss, full fidelity.

## Summary
Frontier now enables teams to import their entire JIRA and Tempo project history without losing a single issue, epic, sprint, comment, attachment, or worklog entry. This migration capability preserves relationships, timestamps, and audit trails, allowing teams to maintain their operational continuity while gaining access to Frontier's modern toolset.

## Problem Statement
Teams evaluating or transitioning to Frontier face a critical blocker: their historical project data — years of issues, sprints, comments, attachments, and time tracking — remains locked in JIRA and Tempo. Manual data entry is infeasible at scale; losing historical context breaks reporting and team knowledge; and platform lock-in fears slow adoption decisions.

## Solution
Frontier provides a built-in, zero-loss migration engine that reads native JIRA and Tempo data structures and maps them directly into Frontier's data model. The migration preserves all relationships, metadata, timestamps, and file attachments in a single, automated operation. Teams can validate the import, then cutover with confidence, knowing their complete operational history is intact and queryable.

## Key Features
- Full issue and epic history import with all custom fields preserved
- Sprint and timeline data migration with historical accuracy
- Complete comment thread migration with author and timestamp fidelity
- Attachment preservation with original file formats and metadata
- Worklog and time-tracking data import from Tempo with entry-level detail
- Automated relationship mapping (issue links, epic hierarchies, sprint assignments)
- Pre-migration validation and data reconciliation reporting
- Rollback capability during cutover validation window

## Customer Benefits
- Eliminate data entry overhead and manual workarounds during platform transition
- Maintain complete audit trail and historical reporting accuracy post-migration
- Reduce cutover risk by preserving all operational context in a single operation
- Enable faster team adoption by keeping familiar project history accessible
- Lower total cost of migration by automating what would otherwise be weeks of manual work
